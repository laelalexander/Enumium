# TS-2 rev B: the GPUs in the tower

Revision B answers the user's request in `inputs/revision-request.md`: put 16 GPUs in each column, engineer how each
card connects, is held and is cooled, design every board in KiCad, and draw the wiring harness. The cooling choice is
**chilled water + Peltier trim**. Everything below is designed and calculated. Nothing has been built or measured.

All engineering files are in `anatomy-tstack-gpu-tower/prototype/`. Paths in sections 2-7 are relative to it; section 9 paths are relative to `anatomy-tstack-gpu-tower/`.

## 1. What is in a column

| | Rev A | Rev B |
|---|---|---|
| GPUs | 4 per sled, 32 per tower, inside node servers | **16 per column, 64 per tower**, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q 96 GB, 300 W blower |
| Host | 8 node sleds | 1 host per column in the plinth arm: ROMED8-2T + EPYC 7543P + 4 x HighPoint Rocket 1628A switch cards |
| GPU connection | motherboard slots | CBP-8 backplane slots, x8 PCIe at Gen4 over one MCIO 8i cable per GPU |
| GPU power | node PSU | 4 x CRPS 2000 W (3 + 1) per column, then CPD-1, straps, 4/0 cables, 14 x 10 mm Cu busbars, then per-slot hot-swap |
| Cooling | air + crown water coil | inlet-side **chilled-water tracing coil** (supply held at dew point + 2 K), **4-TEC Peltier trim** on a split Cu cold block, crown turbines evacuate the plenum |

## 2. How a GPU connects

- **Two CBP-8 backplanes per column** (lower: slots 1-8, upper: slots 9-16). Each is 268 x 434.4 mm, 8 layers, Megtron 6, 2.0 mm.
  - Each slot has a PCIe x16 CEM connector wired x8.
  - Each slot has an MCIO 8i right-angle receptacle with one cable to the host switch card.
  - Each slot has a 12V-2x6 header and a hot-swap controller (MIC2587-1 + CSD17556Q5B, 33 A breaker).
  - Each slot has an INA181 current monitor, a slot 3.3 V buck and PERST# gating.
  - An STM32G474 slot manager powers the slots one at a time, 50 ms apart.
- **Signal integrity.** The estimated Gen4 loss on every link is 20.2-22.8 dB, against a 28 dB budget. That leaves
  5.2-7.8 dB of margin. Cable gauge steps up with length (30, 28 and 26 AWG). See `electrical/ARCHITECTURE.md` §5.
- **Power to each card.** The +12 V and RTN busbars are bolted behind each backplane on M6 x 35 press-fit studs.
  - The stack is: board, 6 mm Cu bush, +12 V bar, 2 mm FR4 spacer, RTN bar.
  - The RTN studs pass through the +12 V bar in 18 mm bushes with PTFE sleeves.
  - Each card is fed by a 150 mm 12V-2x6 cable.

## 3. How a GPU is held

The cards stack at **50.8 mm pitch**, which leaves 10.8 mm of air over each blower intake. Each card is located on
three sides and clamped on the fourth.

1. **Fingers:** the CEM slot takes 60-115 N to seat.
2. **Bracket end:** the card bracket lands on the bracket screw rail (TS2B-205) and is held by a 6-32 thumbscrew at 0.6 N m.
3. **Tail end:** the card tail runs in an EPDM-lined slot of the tail guide comb (TS2B-206). It slides with 5-15 N and has no side play.
4. **Top edge:** a hinged hold-down bar (TS2B-207) carries one EPDM pad (TS2B-208) per card.
   - Each pad squeezes about 1 mm, giving 15-25 N per card.
   - That holds the card in its slot against a 15 g shipping shock.
   - A red latch handle with a GN 617-6 plunger locks the bar to the midspan crossmember.

## 4. How a GPU is cooled (design point 27 C, 50 % RH, full load)

The cooling path, in order:

1. Room air enters through the printed grille and the MERV 8 filter.
2. The **tracing coil** cools it.
   - The coil is 3 rows of 3/8 in Cu, 32 passes, 12 FPI, with 4 circuits per column.
   - The face velocity is 1.30 m/s.
3. The cooled air goes into each card's blower.
4. It leaves through the bracket vents and the windows in the exhaust bulkhead, into the central plenum.
5. The crown turbine (3 x ebm-papst R3G310, N+1, 56 V) holds the plenum at -64 Pa and sends the heat up the 18 in chimney.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Dew point / coolant supply floor | 15.7 C / 17.7 C |
| GPU inlet after the coil | 22.0 C |
| GPU core estimate | 72.1 C (hot room 35 C: 77.7 C; slowdown at 90 C) |
| Coil duty, tower | 5.3 kW (hot room 7.6 kW) |
| Heat into the air, tower | 24.8 kW |
| Air through the turbine | 4733 m3/h, impeller demand 0.76 |
| Tower AC input | 25.5 kW, 35.4 A per 60 A feed |

The coil never condenses. CCM-1 measures the dew point with an SHT45 and lifts the chiller set point (0-10 V)
whenever the supply would fall below dew point + 2 K. The **Peltier trim** module closes the last 1-2 K. It has
4 x 40 mm TECs on the split Cu cold block TS2B-602 in each arm, driven with DC by the TCD-1 (not PWM, to protect
the COP). Each column takes 440 W of cooling from it and rejects 929 W on its hot side. If the chiller trips, the
tower rides through on room air with no slowdown (test AC-THM-03).

## 5. Boards (KiCad, generated by `electrical/kgen/` from `electrical/boards/<board>/design.py`)

| Board | Per tower | Job |
|---|---|---|
| CBP-8 GPU backplane | 8 | 8 GPU slots each; see §2 |
| CPD-1 column power | 4 | takes 4 CRPS; 16 M6 studs to the straps; PMBus; VSB OR-ing; power-on gate |
| CCM-1 cooling controller | 4 | dew point, coil dP, NTCs, leak, flow, pump, chiller run/set point, light bar, doors; power interlock master |
| TCD-1 Peltier driver | 4 | 4 x 14 A synchronous buck into the TECs, hot-side fans |

CPD-1, CCM-1 and TCD-1 pass KiCad DRC with 0 errors and 0 unrouted connections. CBP-8 is routed (PCIe lanes pre-routed as length-matched coupled striplines, the rest autorouted), but 24 DRC items are still open. Most are small leftover stubs and fill islands, and two are slot-sense connections. They are listed in `electrical/boards/cbp8/fab/CBP8-OPEN-ITEMS.md`. Do not order CBP-8 until that list is closed.

Each board has its schematic, layout, netlist check and DRC report in `fab/`. Each also has a RushPCB turnkey zip in
`fab/<board>-rushpcb.zip` with: Gerbers, drill, an Excel BOM (MPN, supplier PN, DNP), a centroid in mm and inches,
assembly drawings with polarity, and fab and test notes. The parts still to confirm against datasheets are listed in
`electrical/part-verification.md`.

## 6. Wiring harness

`electrical/harness/out/` contains:
- TS2B-ELE-101, the harness schematic;
- TS2B-ELE-102, the wire list, with every wire numbered (PDF, Excel, CSV). It covers 76 cables, 127 numbered wires and
  68 connectors, and has a Gen4 loss-budget sheet.

The column RS-485 chain runs CCM-1 → TCD-1 → CPD-1 → CBP-8 lower → CBP-8 upper. The power-on interlock is in
`electrical/ARCHITECTURE.md` §4: GPUs get no 12 V until water is flowing, the coolant is above dew point + 2 K, there
are no leaks, and the doors are shut.

## 7. Drawings and build

- `drawings/TS2B-DWG_set.pdf` holds 33 A3 sheets plus an index. Every made part is drawn in dual units (mm [in]),
  with front, top and side views, a hatched section A-A and an isometric. Each sheet has an ordinate-dimensioned hole
  table with full callouts (size, depth, thread, counterbore), material, finish and tolerances.
- The single-part PDFs are in `drawings/pdf/`, and the DXF cut files (plates, busbars, straps, skins, gaskets, crown flats: 14 parts) are in `drawings/dxf/`.
- `drawings/TS2B-ASM-100_column_build.pdf` is a 12-step illustrated column build, rendered from the CAD. Each step
  lists its parts, torques, checks and tools.
- `bom/TS2B-BOM.xlsx` (and `.csv`) is the tower bill of materials rolled up from the CAD. It has 61 lines, with
  quantities per column and per tower.
- `mechanical/step/` holds the STEP files. `mechanical/assembled/ts2b.glb` and `assembly.json` hold the 1058-part
  assembly.

## 8. Digital twin

`anatomy-tstack-gpu-tower/sim-rev-b/`: run `node server.mjs`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8791/ (or double-click `start.cmd`).

The twin shows:
- the tower from the CAD export, with all 64 GPUs tinted by core temperature;
- the cooling path, power and design tabs;
- faults: chiller off, TEC trim off, impeller loss, CRPS loss, filter clog, feed loss.

Run `node run-tests.mjs` for the 9 acceptance tests. All of them pass.

## 9. Rebuild (Linux or WSL, Python 3.11 + build123d 0.13, KiCad 7, Java 17 for Freerouting)

```
cd prototype/mechanical/cad && python3 build_all.py          # CAD, STEP, GLB, assembly.json
cd prototype/thermal-fluid && python3 calc.py                # sizing
cd prototype/drawings && python3 gen_drawings.py             # 33 sheets + DXF
cd prototype/drawings/asm && python3 make_asm.py             # illustrated build (needs Node + Playwright)
cd prototype/electrical/kgen && python3 build.py <board>     # cbp8 | cpd1 | ccm1 | tcd1
cd prototype/electrical/harness && python3 gen_harness.py
cd prototype/bom && python3 make_bom.py
```

- Confirm the supplier part numbers marked [verify] in `electrical/part-verification.md`.
- Get vendor ratings for the coil and the impellers, then run a first-article airflow test.
- Measure the PCIe channel S-parameters, in place of the loss estimates.
- Close the CBP-8 open DRC items (`electrical/boards/cbp8/fab/CBP8-OPEN-ITEMS.md`).
