# Phase 1 cost summary

Phase 1 equipment comes to **$200,180**: $171,000 bought and $29,180 fabricated in house ($7,855 material and 157 labor hours at $65/h). That is 13% of the Growth plant's $1,512,682.

Buying the fabricated items from an equipment maker would cost roughly $72,950 (2.5 x shop cost, an estimate), so fabricating saves about **$43,770**.
