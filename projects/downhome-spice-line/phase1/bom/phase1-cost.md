# Phase 1 cost summary

Phase 1 equipment comes to **$167,642**: $120,460 bought and $47,182 fabricated in house ($10,018 material and 332.0 labor hours at $65/h). That is 11% of the Growth plant's $1,512,682.

Buying the fabricated items from an equipment maker would cost roughly $117,955 (2.5 x shop cost, an estimate), so fabricating saves about **$70,773**.
