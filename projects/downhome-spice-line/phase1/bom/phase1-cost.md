# Phase 1 cost summary

Phase 1 equipment comes to **$183,900**: $158,080 bought and $25,820 fabricated in house ($6,575 material and 125 labor hours at $65/h). That is 12% of the Growth plant's $1,512,682.

Buying the fabricated items from an equipment maker would cost roughly $64,550 (2.5 x shop cost, an estimate), so fabricating saves about **$38,730**.
