## Notes

- Blender: 6 cu ft chosen (a used Sprout-Waldron B-6 class unit). For the 5 launch blends it needs 7 batches a week instead of 13 at 3 cu ft, and when all 13 seasonings run, the 6 small blends are made every 2 weeks at double size so each batch stays above the 35 % minimum fill.
- Phase 1 is operator-paced: one blend operator and one or two pack operators, on 2 to 3 production days a week.
- Every formula is blended weekly in one batch; finished-goods stock covers sales between runs.
- Rates are estimates for a trained operator; measure them in the first month and update this file.
