## Notes

- Low-volume formulas (< 1,000 lb/week) are blended and packed every second week at double size; finished-goods stock covers the gap (24-month shelf life per the site).
- The weekly wet clean after the wheat campaign is done by the sanitation crew after the Friday shift, so it is outside production minutes.
- Blend batches are made one day ahead of packing so each bag can be QC-released (uniformity CV and micro) before it is opened at the filler.
- Wheat products (boxed dry mixes) run as the last campaign of the week on station B and the last blender campaign; the blender is then wet-cleaned and verified with a gluten swab before Monday.
- Station B handles the wheat mixes, so line A never sees wheat: the gluten-free claim on seasonings is protected by equipment separation at packing, and by cleaning at the shared blender.
- Changeover times are assumptions; measure them at commissioning and update this file.
