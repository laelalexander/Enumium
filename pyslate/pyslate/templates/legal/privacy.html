This policy explains what personal information {{ co.who }} collects when you use our site and order from our shop, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Contact and account details: your name, email, company, phone, and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Order details: your shipping address, what you ordered, quotes, messages and notes, and your order history.
  • Your files: the models, drawings, artwork and photos you upload, and designs you save in the design studio.
  • Payment: card payments are handled by Stripe. We receive a payment confirmation and the card's brand and last four digits, never your full card number.
  • Technical data: our servers log IP addresses, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

Cookies

We use only the cookies the site needs to work: one to keep you signed in, and one to remember the quote you are building. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.

How we use it

  • To quote, make, ship and support your order, and to send you order and shipping updates.
  • To run your account and answer your requests.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
  • To meet our tax, accounting and legal obligations.

We send marketing emails only if you ask for them, and every one has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Stripe, to take payments.
  • USPS, UPS and FedEx, which receive your name, address and phone number to deliver your package.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the site and its data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.

We do not sell your personal information. If {{ co.name }} is ever sold or merged, your information would move to the new owner under this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep account and order records while your account is open and for as long as tax and accounting law requires afterwards (generally seven years). Uploaded files that were never ordered are deleted after 12 months; files for completed orders are kept so we can remake parts, unless you ask us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can see and update your details in your account. You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, or to close your account, and we will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have further rights under state privacy laws; we honor them, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

Security

The site uses HTTPS everywhere, passwords are hashed, and access to order data is limited to our shop staff. No system is perfectly secure, but we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top, and tell you by email if the change is significant.
