How orders work at {{ co.name }}, in plain terms. These policies are part of our Terms of Service.

Orders and payment

  • Instant quotes are valid for 30 days. Orders are paid when placed; production starts once payment clears.
  • Every order is checked by our shop. If something can't be made as quoted, we ask you before changing anything.
  • The minimum order is ${{ '%.2f' % profile.minimum_order }}. Orders of 10 or more of a part get a quantity discount, shown on the quote.

Lead times and rush

Standard orders ship in about {{ profile.lead_days.standard }} business days, rush orders in about {{ profile.lead_days.rush }}. Rush costs more and is shown on the quote. Engineer-quoted and artwork-quoted work has its own schedule in the quote. Your order page shows each part's progress, from queue to machine to packing.

Shipping, pickup and delivery

  • We ship within the United States with USPS, UPS and FedEx, and you can choose local pickup. Each box gets a tracking number, shown on your order page.
  • Check your shipping address before you order. If a package is returned because the address was wrong, we will reship it at your cost.
  • Damaged or lost packages. Tell us within 7 days of delivery (or of the expected delivery date, for a lost package) with photos of the damage and the box. We will remake or refund the affected parts and handle the carrier claim ourselves.

Changes and cancellations

  • Before production starts, you can change or cancel an order for a full refund.
  • After production starts, we can still stop the remaining parts. We refund the order minus the parts and material already made or used, which you can have shipped.
  • Once shipped, an order cannot be cancelled. See returns below.
  • Design services and molds follow the payment and cancellation terms in their written quote.

Returns, remakes and refunds

Everything we make is custom, to your file, so we can't accept returns for a change of mind, a wrong file or a design that doesn't fit your project. We do stand behind our work:

  • If a part or print doesn't match your accepted order, or has a defect in our workmanship, tell us within 14 days of delivery with photos.
  • We will remake it at no cost, or refund it if you prefer or a remake isn't possible. We may ask you to send the part back with a prepaid label we provide.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Color and large format prints

Screens and printers show color differently, so prints are matched as closely as the media allows. Send artwork at print size: 300 dpi for small prints, 150 dpi for large format. We check your file for resolution and bleed and tell you before printing if something looks wrong. Low-resolution artwork you choose to print anyway is not a defect.

Your files and confidentiality

Your files are used only to quote and make your order, seen only by our shop, and never shared or sold. Need a non-disclosure agreement before you send a design? Ask and we will sign one. You can ask us to delete your files once the order is complete.

What we won't make

We don't make items that infringe someone else's rights, are illegal to make or ship, or are intended to deceive. See section 5 of our Terms for the full list.

Accounts

You can order as a guest. With an account you can see all your orders, save quotes and designs, and store your shipping details. You can ask us to close your account at any time; we keep order records as tax and accounting law requires.
