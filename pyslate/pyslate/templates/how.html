// how it works

From file to finished part.

Two ways in: upload a file for an instant price, or send us a design request. Both end with parts made in our shop.

With a file instant quote

  1. Upload

    Send STL, 3MF, OBJ or PLY files for 3D parts, or DXF for flat parts. Up to 20 files at once.

  2. We check every part

    We measure size, volume and thickness, detect flat parts, and flag open meshes, thin features and parts too big for our machines.

  3. We pick the machine

    Flat parts in a sheet material go to the laser: the xTool X1, M2 or F2, or the MetalFab for steel. Everything else goes to a Bambu Lab printer.

  4. You see the price

    Material, machine time and setup, line by line. Change material, quantity or lead time and the price updates. Orders from 10 parts get a discount.

  5. Order and track

    Pay online. Your parts go into our queue, and your order page shows each step: in queue, making, finishing and shipped.

With an idea engineer quoted

  1. Tell us what you need

    Describe the system or part, and attach sketches, photos or existing drawings.

  2. An engineer replies

    Usually within two business days, with questions or a fixed quote.

  3. We design and document

    Models, blueprints and schematics, reviewed with you until they're right.

  4. We build it

    The parts go into the same production queue as instant orders.
