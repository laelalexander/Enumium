    Inputs

    No gamepad. Press any button on a connected controller.
    Keyboard and gamepad map

    Joint mode: Q/A yaw, W/S shoulder, E/D elbow, R/F wrist, T/G gripper.
    Cartesian mode: arrows X/Y, PgUp/PgDn Z, R/F pitch.
    Space STOP, H home all, P record pose.
    Gamepad: left stick yaw + shoulder (or X/Y), right stick elbow + wrist (or Z/pitch), triggers grip, A record, B STOP, Y home, start toggles mode.

    Each channel is one DM542 driver and stepper. Changes are sent as V ch steps/s accel. Pulses/rev and current must match the driver DIP switches.
